# Creative AI Prompts

An open-source resource for designers, filmmakers, and content creators.

## Image Generation Prompt

Create a professional advertising visual with cinematic lighting, realistic textures, balanced composition, and a clear visual hierarchy. Maintain consistent brand colors and avoid unnecessary visual clutter.

## Video Generation Prompt

Create a cinematic sequence with smooth camera movement, realistic motion, consistent character identity, and professional lighting.

## Brand Identity Prompt

Develop a distinctive visual identity with a cohesive color palette, typography system, logo direction, and practical brand applications.

## Social Media Prompt

Develop a creative social media campaign with a strong hook, compelling storytelling, and a clear call to action.

## Contributions

Designers, developers, and content creators are welcome to contribute new prompts, workflows, and improvements.
